Paperkeep Document Redactor · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Our privacy stance fits in one sentence: your files never leave your computer.

Paperkeep Document Redactor (“the app”) is a Windows desktop application that runs entirely on your machine. This policy explains what data the app touches, where that data lives, and what we never do.

1. We collect no data

The app has no accounts, embeds no analytics or advertising SDKs, and never uploads your files or collects usage data. Documents you add, detection results, and exported files are stored only on your own disk, and we have no way to access them.

2. Data stored on your machine

  • App settings (window position, redaction style, custom keywords) are kept in your local user profile;
  • A run log is kept at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Paperkeep\paperkeep.log for troubleshooting only;
  • License status (whether the app has been purchased) is kept at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Paperkeep\license.dat, encrypted with Windows DPAPI and bound to this machine. It contains no personally identifying information.
  • None of this is uploaded or shared with anyone.

About license status

When the app starts (and when more than 45 days have passed since the last check), it asks Windows for this app's Microsoft Store license status in order to tell whether it has been purchased. That check is performed by system components and never involves the documents you process, and sends no usage data back to us. The rest of the time it is fully offline, and your files are read and written only on your own machine.

3. Third-party components

Components such as the OCR engine run offline on your machine. No page images or recognized text are ever sent to any server.

4. Children's privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
